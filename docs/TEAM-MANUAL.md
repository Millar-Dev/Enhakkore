# Enhakkore — Team Member Manual

For team members who are not the owner: customer support, the travel, events,
Explore and community teams, and organisers working with us. The full owner's manual
is [ADMIN-MANUAL.md](ADMIN-MANUAL.md) (also inside the control centre under **Help &
manual**).

What you can see and do depends on your **role**. If a menu item is missing or a
button says *You do not have permission*, ask the owner to change your role. Never
share your password or sign-in code with anyone.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open `/admin` on your phone or computer and sign in with the email and the
   one-time password the owner sent you.
2. Change the password straight away (menu → your name → password).
3. Turn on **two-factor sign-in** (menu → **My two-factor sign-in**). It is required
   for anyone with finance, staff or settings access, and recommended for everyone.

If you lose your phone, tell the owner at once so they can sign you out everywhere.

## 2. Your daily list

Start at **Today** in the control centre:

- **Hotel enquiries** and **Restaurant reservations** — reply within the day.
- **Trip applications** — approve, decline, record verified payments.
- **Event registrations** — confirm and check in.
- **Organiser applications** (Experiences team) — see section 5.
- **Reviews & reports** — reported chat messages and reviews.

## 3. Enquiries and reservations (support, Explore team)

A request is never a booking until the business accepts it.

1. Open the enquiry. The reference (ENQ-…) is what the customer has.
2. Contact the business. Use **Reply on WhatsApp** to talk to the customer.
3. Move the status honestly: *Contacted → Awaiting business response → Alternative
   offered → Confirmed* or *Declined / Unsuccessful*.
4. Only set **Confirmed** when the business has really accepted. At that moment the
   customer gets an **Enhakkore Pass** in their account (for hotels and restaurants).

Do not promise refunds, deposits, prices or availability the business has not given
you in writing. Customers who press *send* twice get the same reference back — there
is no duplicate to clean up.

## 4. Checking people in (events, activities, trips)

Use **Check-in scanner & passes** (in the menu, or **My account → Check-in scanner**).

1. Choose the event.
2. **Scan with camera** (Chrome on Android) or type the **PASS-…** reference.
3. Follow the result:
   - **Valid — checked in**: let them in.
   - **Already checked in**: the pass was used before; the first time is shown.
   - **Needs review**: someone typed a reference for a QR pass — check who they are,
     then press **Identity confirmed — check in**.
   - **Cancelled / Expired / Not found**: do not admit on this pass. Check their
     booking or call the organiser.
4. **Find someone on the list** by name, @username or reference when phones fail.

**No internet at the venue?** Before you leave, switch on **Offline mode** and press
**Download offline list** (valid 12 hours). Results offline are provisional. Press
**Sync** as soon as you are back online and look at anything flagged.

The QR code holds no personal details. Never ask anyone for an ID number to put into
the website.

## 5. Organiser applications (Experiences team)

1. Open the application → **Mark under review**.
2. Call or video-call the applicant and see an ID. In **How identity was checked**
   write what you did — *never* the ID number, and never upload a document.
3. Approve only the categories you are comfortable with. Large, paid or physically
   risky experiences need more care.
4. Use **Ask for more information** with a clear note, or **Reject** politely.

Approved organisers still submit every experience for review. When you review a
submission, check: public meeting place (never a home address), realistic capacity,
honest price, an **Entry check** that fits (None for ordinary free activities), and
the rules and safety notes.

## 6. Group chats and moderation

Trip groups open for confirmed travellers. Event, activity and community chats open
when the organiser switches **Group chat** on. As a moderator you can pin, remove
messages, remove a participant (with a recorded reason) and close the chat.

- Read reported messages under **Reviews & reports** and act the same day.
- Remove anything abusive, unsafe, or that shares someone's phone number, address or
  ID. Members can block each other themselves.
- Do not use chats for private conversations with members.

## 7. Communities

Private and paid communities send join **requests**. Open the community → **Members
& announcements** → **Approve** or **Decline**. For paid memberships, online payment
is not switched on: approve only after the payment has been arranged and recorded by
finance. Membership fees are separate from the cost of any event.

## 8. Privacy rules for everyone

- Members are shown by **@username**. Phone numbers and emails are for the team only
  — never paste them into chats, public pages or screenshots.
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, prices, attendance or impact figures. Example
  content must stay marked **DEMO**.
- Do not tell anyone a feature works if it does not. *Enhakkore Nearby* is not
  working on the website yet; online payment is not switched on.
- Every change you make is recorded in the audit log with your name.

## 9. When something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A member's QR "does not work" | Ask them to reopen the pass — if the date or place changed a new code was issued. Otherwise check in by reference. |
| Camera scanner will not start | Type the reference. Camera scanning needs Chrome on Android. |
| Enquiry not on WhatsApp | It is still saved. Reply from the enquiry page. |
| Someone is behaving badly in a chat | Remove the message, remove the person if needed (give a reason), tell the owner. |
| You clicked something by mistake | Tell the owner; most changes can be restored from *Change history*. |
